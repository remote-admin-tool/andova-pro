## 📱 How It Works

Getting started with Andova Pro is simple and takes just a few steps:

1. **Create Your Account** — Sign up securely with your email and password to access your powerful dashboard.

1. **Add a Device** — Name the target device and generate a secure, one-time pairing code.

1. **Pair the Device** — Enter the pairing code inside the Andova app on the target Android phone.

1. **Monitor & Control** — Once paired, you can track live location, take camera snaps, view keylogs, and remotely manage the device from anywhere.
